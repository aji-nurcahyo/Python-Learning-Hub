## Modul Silabus Terstruktur
### Modul 1: Mathematical Essentials for ML [Specialization]
Aljabar linier, kalkulus diferensial, dan probabilitas terapan dalam Python.

### Modul 2: Supervised Learning (Regression & Classification) [Specialization]
Linear models, Decision Trees, Random Forests, dan Support Vector Machines.

### Modul 3: Unsupervised Learning & Dimensionality Reduction [Specialization]
Clustering K-Means, DBSCAN, dan reduksi dimensi Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

### Modul 4: Model Evaluation, Validation & Hyperparameter Tuning [Specialization]
Cross-validation, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix, dan Grid/Random Search.

### Modul 5: Capstone: Model Prediksi Sinyal & Anomali Jaringan [Advanced Project]
Melatih dan menguji model machine learning prediktif dengan dataset sensor jaringan telekomunikasi.
